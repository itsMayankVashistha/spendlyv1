Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Spendly is a personal expense tracking application. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and what you can do about it.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what the application needs in order to work:

  • Account details — the name and email address you provide when registering, along with a securely hashed password.
  • Expense records — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter yourself.
  • Basic technical data — information such as your session cookie, which keeps you signed in between page loads.

We do not ask for bank credentials, card numbers, or access to your financial accounts.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used to provide the service and nothing else. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Create and secure your account, and keep you signed in.
  • Store your expenses and display them back to you.
  • Generate the summaries, category breakdowns, and date filters shown in the app.
  • Diagnose problems and keep the service running reliably.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense records are stored in the application's database. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We keep your data for as long as your account is active, and we take reasonable technical measures to protect it.

You can delete individual expense records at any time. If you ask us to close your account, the associated data is removed. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please keep your own copies of anything you cannot afford to lose.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly relies on a small number of third parties to operate — for example, hosting providers and web font services that deliver the typefaces used on these pages. These providers may process limited technical data, such as your IP address, as part of serving the application.

We do not share your expense records or account details with third parties for their own purposes, and we only disclose information where required by law.

5. Your Choices

You can review and update your account details from within the application, export or delete your expense records, and stop using the service at any time. Most browsers also let you block or clear cookies, though doing so will sign you out of Spendly.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "last updated" date above. Continuing to use Spendly after changes take effect means you accept the revised policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or about how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact details provided in the application. See also our Terms and Conditions.
